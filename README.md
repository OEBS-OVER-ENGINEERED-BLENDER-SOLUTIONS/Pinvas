# Pinvas

A reference board inside Blender. Drop, paste or import pictures onto a board in the 3D View sidebar, arrange them, and keep them beside your model while you work.

![A tidied Pinvas board of reference pictures in Blender's 3D View sidebar](https://docs.oebs-studios.com/pinvas/free/assets/board-tidy-hero.png)

## What it does

- **Put pictures on the board:** drop picture files from your file manager, press **Ctrl+V** over the board to paste a screenshot or a copied picture, or click **Import**.
- **Arrange them:** select, move, scale and turn pictures with the mouse, or with **G**, **S** and **R** as elsewhere in Blender. **X** takes a picture off the board, and **Ctrl+Z** brings it back.
- **Tidy the board:** **Ctrl+P** packs the pictures into one tight block, shaped to fit the board.
- **Greyscale and see-through:** judge values in greys, or compare one picture over another, from the right-click **Appearance** menu.
- **Pick a colour:** press **E** and click a picture to copy that colour to the clipboard as a hex code such as `#BC4C36`.
- **Undock:** on Windows, open the board in a window of its own and drag it to a second monitor; on other systems **Float** floats it over the viewport.

Boards are saved inside your .blend file.

## Install

Requires Blender 4.5 or newer (tested on 4.5, 5.0, 5.1, 5.2 and 5.3).

1. Download `Pinvas-v1.0.1.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/OEBS-OVER-ENGINEERED-BLENDER-SOLUTIONS/Pinvas/releases/latest). Leave it zipped.
2. In Blender, open **Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions**, open the menu at the top right and choose **Install from Disk...**.
3. Choose the ZIP. Blender installs and enables **Pinvas**.

## Quick start

1. Hover over a 3D View, press **N** and open the **Pinvas** tab.
2. Click **Import** on the board's bar and choose some pictures, or paste one with **Ctrl+V**.
3. Drag a picture to move it, drag a corner handle to scale it, and drag the round handle above it to turn it.
4. Press **Ctrl+P** to tidy the board and **Home** to see every picture.

Scroll to zoom and middle-drag to pan. Keys act only while the mouse is over the board; everywhere else Blender behaves as usual.

## Pinvas Pro

[Pinvas Pro](https://docs.oebs-studios.com/pinvas/) adds video, drawing on your references, frames, notes and Send to Scene. It's sold on Superhive, where this edition is also listed for $1 (the lowest price Superhive allows) if you'd like to support it. Your boards carry over when you upgrade. Only one of the two can be enabled at a time.
